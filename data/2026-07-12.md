### [1] [Prismata: Confining Cross-Site Prompt Injection in Web Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08147)
*Corban Villa, Alp Eren Ozdarendeli, Sijun Tan, Raluca Ada Popa*

  <summary>Details</summary>

Method: Prismata uses dynamic trust derivation to assign permission labels to page content, with structural confinement guarantees inspired by classical integrity models, and enforces these labels by redacting content and restricting agent capabilities without requiring developer annotations.

Abstract: Autonomous web agents promise to automate everyday browsing tasks, but inherit one of the web's oldest attack surfaces. The core challenge is that deriving a task-specific security policy requires reasoning over page structure that is entangled with the attacker's content.
