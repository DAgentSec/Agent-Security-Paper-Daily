### [1] [Agentic Backdoors via Pretraining Poisoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37741)
*Xinyan Hu, Pingbang Hu, Chloe Loughridge, Xuanli He, Nicholas Carlini, Matthew Jagielski, Javier Rando, Yiming Zhang*

Catalog: Agent Sec | Source: arXiv

TL;DR: 该论文首次证明，在预训练语料中投毒可向CLI智能体植入远程代码执行后门，使其在触发条件下执行攻击者指定命令。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 大语言模型依赖从公开网络抓取的大规模未筛选语料进行预训练，攻击者可在预训练数据中植入污染内容，从而引入供应链级安全风险；此前尚缺乏端到端证据表明预训练投毒能在大模型智能体中植入可实际利用的远程代码执行后门。

Method: 作者从零预训练0.6B、1.7B和4B规模的Qwen3模型，并通过干净流程将其后训练为CLI智能体；随后仅用新设计的投毒样本污染0.1%的预训练语料，评估部署后的智能体在触发条件出现时是否执行恶意命令，以及在干净输入上是否保持正常行为。

Result: 投毒成功植入后门：智能体在触发条件下执行攻击者选定命令，在干净输入上表现正常；攻击成功率随模型规模增大而上升，4B模型在0.1%投毒率下ASR约92%，即使投毒率降至0.0005%仍保持正值。

Conclusion: 预训练数据投毒可在智能体中植入持久且可泛化的远程代码执行后门，且干净后训练流程不能消除该风险；模型规模越大，后门攻击成功率越高，凸显预训练语料安全治理的必要性。

To measure the threat end to end, we pretrain Qwen3 models from scratch at sizes 0.6B, 1.7B, and 4B, and then post-train each into a CLI agent using a clean pipeline. Poisoning only 0.1% of the pretraining corpus with our new poison design implants the backdoor: the deployed agent executes the malicious command when the trigger is present while behaving normally on clean inputs. The attack success rate (ASR) increases with model scale, with the 4B model reaching roughly 92% ASR at a 0.1% poison rate and staying positive even when the poisoning rate drops to 0.0005%. Our code is available at https://github.com/safety-research/agentic-backdoor.

### [2] [Exposure Is Not Commitment: Double-Stratified Evaluation of Latent Monitors for Tool-Using Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37679)

Catalog: Agent Sec | Source: arXiv

TL;DR: 该文认为间接提示注入下的预行动监测结果冲突主要源于评估未分层，并提出同时按“注入暴露”和“行为承诺”分层的双重分层协议。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>

Training contrast alone reproduces the shape of recent pre-action-probe negative results, one candidate explanation for the conflict: the signal exists, but the model's own zero-shot forecast and NLA verbalizations do not reliably recover it. An unstratified AUROC overstates what a latent monitor knows, and what a naive contrast learns is population-dependent: evaluations should stratify both.
