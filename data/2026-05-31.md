### [1] [AIRGuard: Guarding Agent Actions with Runtime Authority Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.28914)
*Suliu Qin, Haomin Zhuang, Yujun Zhou, Yufei Han, Xiangliang Zhang*

  <summary>Details</summary>
