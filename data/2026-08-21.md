### [1] [Frequency-Aware Continual Learning for Smart Contract Vulnerability Detection with Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19680)

Third, the resulting multiplicity of adapters must be consolidated into a single model, since task identity is unknown at inference time.
