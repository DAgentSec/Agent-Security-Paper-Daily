### [1] [Agentic Backdoors via Pretraining Poisoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37741)
*Xinyan Hu, Pingbang Hu, Chloe Loughridge, Xuanli He, Nicholas Carlini, Matthew Jagielski, Javier Rando, Yiming Zhang*

Catalog: Agent Sec | Source: arXiv

TL;DR: 该论文首次证明，在预训练语料中投毒可向CLI智能体植入远程代码执行后门，使其在触发条件下执行攻击者指定命令。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 大语言模型依赖从公开网络爬取的大规模未筛选语料进行预训练，攻击者可在其中植入投毒内容，从而形成供应链级安全风险；此前缺乏端到端证据表明预训练投毒能转化为智能体中的远程代码执行后门。

Method: 作者从零预训练0.6B、1.7B和4B的Qwen3模型，并通过干净流程将其后训练为CLI智能体；随后用新设计的投毒样本污染0.1%的预训练语料，测量部署后智能体在触发条件下的攻击成功率（ASR）及干净输入上的行为。

Result: 投毒可成功植入后门：触发时智能体执行恶意命令，干净输入下表现正常；ASR随模型规模增大而上升，4B模型在0.1%投毒率下达到约92%，即使投毒率降至0.0005%仍保持为正。

To measure the threat end to end, we pretrain Qwen3 models from scratch at sizes 0.6B, 1.7B, and 4B, and then post-train each into a CLI agent using a clean pipeline. The attack success rate (ASR) increases with model scale, with the 4B model reaching roughly 92% ASR at a 0.1% poison rate and staying positive even when the poisoning rate drops to 0.0005%. Our code is available at https://github.com/safety-research/agentic-backdoor.

### [2] [Exposure Is Not Commitment: Double-Stratified Evaluation of Latent Monitors for Tool-Using Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37679)
*Rishabh Sinha*

Catalog: Agent Sec | Source: arXiv

An unstratified AUROC overstates what a latent monitor knows, and what a naive contrast learns is population-dependent: evaluations should stratify both.
